# Corrections & Feedback / সংশোধন ও মতামত

Thank you for helping improve ML Research Handbook by MD Khalid Hasan. Reports in English or Bangla are welcome; no Git experience is needed.

## Report an error

Open [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/khalidhasan7343/ml-research-handbook/issues), choose **New issue**, then **Correction report / সংশোধনের প্রতিবেদন**. If the form is unavailable, a regular issue with the details below is sufficient.

1. Choose the issue category: factual accuracy, mathematics, reference, translation, clarity, typography, or accessibility.
2. Identify the section and edition (Markdown, PDF, or DOCX). Include a page number when relevant; page numbers may differ by format.
3. Quote the relevant text or equation briefly and explain the problem.
4. Suggest a correction if you have one. You can report a problem without knowing the answer.
5. Provide a reliable supporting reference where possible: the original paper, official documentation, or a relevant textbook. Include a DOI, stable link, page, theorem, or equation number so the author can check it.

## Translation and clarity

For English–Bangla translation issues, include both the wording you found and the meaning you believe was intended. Explain whether the concern is terminology, ambiguity, grammar, or a difference between the two languages. A short suggested wording is welcome.

বাংলা বা ইংরেজিতে সংশোধনের প্রস্তাব জানাতে পারেন। সেকশন নম্বর, সংস্করণ, সংক্ষিপ্ত উদ্ধৃতি এবং সমস্যার বিবরণ দিন। অনুবাদের সমস্যায় সংশ্লিষ্ট শব্দ বা বাক্যের উদ্দেশ্য স্পষ্ট করুন; সম্ভব হলে নির্ভরযোগ্য সূত্র বা সংশোধনের পরামর্শ যোগ করুন।

## How feedback is reviewed

The author will assess the report against the manuscript and supporting sources, request clarification when needed, and consider the effect on each edition. Appropriate corrections will be incorporated into future updates and recorded in the changelog. A report may remain open while the evidence is checked; submitting a suggestion does not guarantee its adoption or a particular response time.
