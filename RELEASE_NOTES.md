## ML Research Handbook — Final Edition (2026)

The Final Edition of the bilingual (English · বাংলা) handbook on machine learning, research paper reading, critical analysis, research gap identification — and the whole path from a research question to a published paper.

**📖 Download `ML_Research_Handbook.pdf` below** (199 pages, free).

### What's inside

| Part | Chapters | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| I · Machine Learning Foundations | 1–6 | Vocabulary, project pipeline, data quality, algorithms, metrics, experimental design |
| II · Reading Research Papers | 7–9 | Paper anatomy, the three-pass method, the literature matrix |
| III · Critical Analysis | 10–12 | 14-dimension critique, red flags, reproducibility |
| IV · Research Gaps, Questions and Novelty | 13–17 | 16 gap types, verifying a gap, from gap to research question, novelty |
| V · Practice | 18–19 | AlexNet case study, worked numerical examples |
| VI · Beyond the Core | 20–21 | Modern ML concepts, fairness and research ethics |
| **VII · Research in the Age of LLMs** | 22–25 | Reading LLM papers, AI tools in research, Bangla and low-resource ML, a case study of "Attention Is All You Need" |
| **VIII · From Question to Publication** | 26–29 | Research proposals, running a study with sound statistics, writing the paper, submission and peer review |
| Appendices | A–E | Templates (including a proposal form and rebuttal planner), formula sheet, glossary, cheat sheet, answers to exercises |

- Every key idea is explained in English, then again in natural Bangla.
- A Bangla sentiment-analysis mini-project runs through Chapters 24–29, from finding a gap to answering reviewers.
- Chapters 22–29 end with exercises; answers are in Appendix E.
- 133 references in IEEE style, each new one checked against its primary source.
- The full Typst source is in the repository, and `make` rebuilds this exact PDF.
